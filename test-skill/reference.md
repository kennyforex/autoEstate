# Car Wash Service Reference

## Service Descriptions

### Basic Exterior Wash (`basic`)
- High-pressure rinse
- Foam cannon application
- Hand wash with microfiber mitts
- Wheel and tire cleaning
- Rinse and air dry
- Duration: ~30 minutes

### Interior Cleaning (`interior`)
- Vacuum all seats and carpets
- Dashboard and console wipe-down
- Window cleaning (interior)
- Air freshener
- Duration: ~45 minutes

### Full Detail (`full`)
- Everything in Basic Exterior + Interior
- Clay bar treatment
- Hand wax application
- Tire dressing
- Chrome polishing
- Duration: ~2-3 hours

### Engine Bay Cleaning (`engine`)
- Degreasing spray
- Low-pressure rinse
- Dressing application
- Duration: ~30 minutes
